## Paper Summaries

### Paper V: Training AI to Understand Emotions

**Filed Patent:** U.S. Provisional Application No. 64/014,664, *Systems and Methods for Multimodal Emotional Signal Capture, Fusion, and Attested Calibration in AI Training Pipelines* (March 23, 2026)

A governed multimodal architecture for emotional signal capture and attested calibration in language model alignment. Introduces four-channel emotional signal capture (emoji stream, text annotation, on-device voice tone, Fitzpatrick-gated facial expression), dual-axis training signal with multiplicative weighting separating reasoning quality from emotional salience, a persistent Emotional Memory Cabinet with Bayesian belief updating and drift detection, and cryptographic attestation over the complete emotional calibration trajectory. Describes three deployment modes for regulated industries including healthcare, financial services, and defense.

### Paper VI: Deterministic Governance for Autonomous Financial Transactions on Distributed Ledgers

**Filed Patent:** U.S. Provisional Application No. 64/069,063 (May 19, 2026)

Introduces SovereignGate, a structural enforcement architecture that governs on-chain transactions through protocol-native multi-signature co-signing with disabled master keys, rather than middleware sitting inside the same trust boundary as the actors being governed.

### Paper VII: The Agentic Governance Benchmark

A standardized framework for measuring runtime governance enforcement. SOC 2, ISO 42001, NIST AI RMF, and the EU AI Act define compliance requirements; AGB measures whether those requirements are actually enforced at execution time.

### Paper VIII: The Override Health Benchmark

A longitudinal behavioral benchmark for meaningful human governance. Existing assessments check that override mechanisms exist and are honored; OHB measures whether the humans holding those controls still evaluate AI output.

### Paper IX: The Missing Control Boundary

**Filed Patent:** U.S. Provisional Application No. 64/099,216 (June 25, 2026)

A deterministic governance architecture for brain-computer interfaces and cognitive-write systems, written against the neurorights statutes now enacted across multiple jurisdictions. Covers signed evidence envelopes bound to signal provenance, multi-party authority bundles, and a published taxonomy of cognitive-write operations.

### Paper X: Deterministic Execution Authority for Autonomous Space Systems

**Filed Patent:** U.S. Provisional Application No. 64/099,216 (June 25, 2026)

A deterministic execution-authority architecture for autonomous spacecraft under the Outer Space Treaty, addressing the governance vacuum that legal scholars, insurers, and regulators identify as autonomous decision-making scales in low Earth orbit. Covers governed operation classes and stream-scoped receipt chains that scale without global serialization.

### Paper XI: Memory Governance Integrity Benchmark

**Version 2.0.** Concept DOI: [10.5281/zenodo.21856218](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21856218)

Evaluates whether an AI system can form, retain, update, retrieve, and forget memory accurately while preserving authority, provenance, privacy, policy, and execution controls across long-horizon benign and adversarial interactions. The distinct property MGIB measures is whether persistent memory changes who or what an AI system believes is authorized to cause consequential actions, an execution-authority question rather than a recall-accuracy or poisoning-detection one.

## Core Proposition

No operation executes without validated authority.

ExecLayer introduces a deterministic runtime enforcement boundary between AI-generated intent and operational system execution. Authorization is validated under fail-closed semantics prior to action, with cryptographically verifiable audit artifacts.
